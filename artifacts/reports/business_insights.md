# Business Insights Report

Overall churn rate: **13.6%**

## Key observations
- Customers on Month-to-month churn the most at 18.2%. That is well above the overall churn rate of 13.6%.
- Very new customers are the riskiest segment. The 0-6 month group churns at 32.3%, while the 73+ month group drops to 0.0%.
- Fiber optic users churn more than the rest. The churn rate for Fiber optic customers is 23.3%.
- Customers paying through Electronic check are noticeably less sticky, with churn at 15.7%.
- Paperless billing is associated with higher churn: 14.6% for paperless customers versus 12.0% for non-paperless customers.
- Customers in the highest monthly charge band churn more than the cheapest band, roughly 28.6% versus 2.5%.

## Practical retention moves
- Push month-to-month customers toward 12-month or 24-month plans with a small incentive, because contract length is one of the clearest churn separators.
- Treat the first six months as a retention window. A better onboarding flow, check-in emails, and usage nudges will pay off here.
- Review fiber optic accounts with support issues or high charges first. They are expensive customers to lose and often leave for avoidable reasons.
- Make payment and billing simpler. Autopay incentives and clearer invoices can reduce churn on billing-sensitive segments.
- Target high monthly-charge customers with save offers before renewal, not after cancellation.
